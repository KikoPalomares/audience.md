# Principles

## 1. Human-readable first

A person should understand an AudienceMD file without a tool, build step, account, or schema viewer.

## 2. Useful beats complete

A short, concrete file is better than a long, decorative one. The standard should encourage clarity, not bureaucracy.

## 3. Evidence and assumptions are different

Audience context often begins with guesses. That is fine if they are labeled. AudienceMD should make confidence visible.

## 4. Audiences are situational

People are not only demographics. Their context, goals, constraints, and timing often matter more than age, location, or job title.

## 5. Anti-goals matter

Good audience definition says who not to chase and which tactics are unacceptable.

## 6. Agents should be constrained, not merely inspired

The file should help AI systems decide what to do and what not to do.

## 7. The format should travel

AudienceMD should work in a Git repo, a campaign folder, a content calendar, a design project, or a product workspace.

## 8. The core should stay small

Extensions are welcome, but the base standard should remain easy to read, copy, and maintain.
