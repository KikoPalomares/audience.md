# AUDIENCE.md — Independent AI strategy newsletter

## Audience name

Operators and founders who want practical AI adoption guidance without hype.

## Summary

The newsletter serves busy professionals responsible for making AI useful inside real businesses. They are skeptical of both doom and hype and want grounded examples, workflows, and decisions they can apply.

## Primary audiences

### 1. Business operators implementing AI

People inside companies who need to evaluate, pilot, and operationalize AI tools.

**Needs**

- clear use cases
- risk-aware implementation advice
- examples that map to daily work

**Constraints**

- limited time
- internal skepticism
- compliance and privacy concerns

**Current alternatives or behaviors**

- following AI Twitter/LinkedIn
- reading vendor blogs
- experimenting ad hoc with tools

## Secondary audiences

- founders exploring AI-native products
- consultants advising clients on AI workflows
- technically curious executives

## Jobs to be done / desired outcomes

- When a new AI tool appears, they want to know whether it matters for their work.
- When pitching AI internally, they want sober arguments and examples.

## Pains, anxieties, and constraints

- fatigue from constant AI announcements
- fear of looking uninformed
- concern about privacy, hallucinations, and vendor lock-in

## Motivations

- stay ahead without wasting time
- make better operational decisions
- build credibility internally

## Decision criteria

- concise
- specific
- honest about limits
- no recycled hype threads

## Language and tone

Practical, sharp, slightly opinionated. Avoid breathless “this changes everything” framing.

## Anti-goals and exclusions

- not for people seeking beginner prompt lists only
- not for pure research coverage
- do not promote unsafe data practices

## Evidence

- Market observation: AI newsletters are abundant, but many readers complain about low signal. Confidence: medium.

## Assumptions

- Readers prefer fewer, better issues over daily noise.
- Implementation examples outperform news summaries.

## Open questions

- Should the newsletter prioritize strategy, workflows, or tool reviews?
- What depth is ideal for busy operators?
