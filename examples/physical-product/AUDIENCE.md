# AUDIENCE.md — Ergonomic travel keyboard

## Audience name

Remote workers who travel often and refuse to compromise on typing comfort.

## Summary

This audience works from hotels, trains, cafes, coworking spaces, and short-term rentals. They care about portability, comfort, durability, and setup speed more than novelty.

## Primary audiences

### 1. Frequent-travel knowledge workers

Writers, engineers, consultants, founders, and operators who type for hours while traveling.

**Needs**

- compact carry size
- comfortable key feel
- reliable Bluetooth or wired mode
- fast setup on multiple devices

**Constraints**

- luggage space
- battery anxiety
- unpredictable workspaces
- price sensitivity compared with normal keyboards

**Current alternatives or behaviors**

- laptop keyboard only
- folding keyboards with poor feel
- carrying a full-size keyboard reluctantly

## Secondary audiences

- digital nomads
- conference-heavy executives
- accessibility-conscious users needing better ergonomics

## Jobs to be done / desired outcomes

- When working away from home, they want a keyboard that makes serious work feel normal.
- When packing light, they want comfort without bulky gear.

## Pains, anxieties, and constraints

- wrist pain from laptop keyboards
- unreliable Bluetooth pairing
- cheap travel keyboards feel bad
- fear of damaging expensive gear in transit

## Motivations

- protect focus and comfort
- maintain productivity anywhere
- invest in a durable tool

## Decision criteria

- typing feel
- portability
- battery life
- build quality
- device switching
- warranty and repairability

## Language and tone

Concrete and tactile. Show real travel setups, dimensions, weight, and comparisons. Avoid vague “work from anywhere” clichés.

## Anti-goals and exclusions

- not for gamers seeking RGB features
- not for buyers who only want the cheapest keyboard
- do not overpromise medical benefits

## Evidence

- Product category observation: travel keyboards often trade comfort for portability. Confidence: medium.

## Assumptions

- Frequent travelers will pay a premium for real typing comfort.
- Repairability and durability can differentiate the product.

## Open questions

- What weight threshold feels acceptable?
- Which layout compromises are tolerable?
